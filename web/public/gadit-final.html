ephemeral

Meanings 2
1
Lasting for a very short time — present briefly, then gone.
The beauty of cherry blossoms is famously ephemeral, lasting only a week each spring.
He learned that fame is ephemeral, but craft endures.
2
Of plants or insects with a very short life cycle — the mayfly, the desert bloom.
After the first rain in seven years, the desert bloomed with ephemeral flowers.
Word Origin
Language
Ancient Greek
Originally meant
"lasting only a day"

Ancient physicians used the word for fevers that came and went within a single day. By the 16th century, English writers had borrowed it for anything fleeting — flowers, beauty, life itself.

Notes for the next step

The hierarchy is now typographic, not chromatic.

The page reads in two voices: Meanings is the dictionary entry — black ink on warm cream, no card, no decoration, the way a Penguin Classics page would treat the primary text. Word Origin · Visual · Take it further are the marginalia — colored stations beneath the main column, the way a printed reference book uses sidebars and footnote color. A returning user gets both: instant scan-to-color for the support sections, and undisturbed reading for the core meaning.

The Word Origin block is now a structured etymology entry, not a paragraph: language, source form, breakdown into meaningful parts, original meaning. A short historical note follows when one is worth telling. This is the order our community of language teachers actually wants the information in — they ask "where did this word come from" before they ask "what's its story."

Three small choices that have been resolved in this final pass:

1 · In Simple TermsLives as a tile inside Take it further. On mobile, the first scroll stays focused on Meanings + Word Origin + Visual. Anyone who needs a simpler explanation taps in.
2 · Progress signalBoth views show a quiet "Saved · 2 days ago" line at the top when the word is already in the user's Word Book. Functional history, never a count or a streak. Hidden entirely on first read.
3 · Desktop action placementNo sticky bottom bar on desktop. The Save button lives top-right of the header — the way a long-form magazine puts Subscribe there. Mobile keeps the thumb-zone bar.
