## South Africa facts
- **Schools:** 2,507 independent schools, +70 a year; 931 in Gauteng, 341 in Western Cape; Gauteng +33% over a decade.
- **Internet:** 78%+ access.
- **Outcomes:** only ~17% of grade 5 pupils reach minimum math proficiency (UNESCO GEM 2025), so a school must SEE an outcome or it won't renew.
- **TIMSS gap:** Afrikaans-medium schools score 56 points higher in grade 5 math (397 vs 341) and 97 in science. They're a better-funded audience, which is DeepSeek's argument for Afrikaans.
- **Language policy:** MTbBE expands mother-tongue-based teaching in math and science; Zulu MTbBE expands to 3,582 schools in grade 5 in KZN. The BELA Bill changes language policy in public schools, so watch it.
- **Competitors and ecosystem:** iSchoolAfrica (since 2009, townships) and the Injini / Mastercard Foundation EdTech Fellowship. Injini is an accelerator worth applying to for exposure, funding and a network.
- **Fees:** ISASA mid-fee schools charge R60k–85k a year.

## Languages
English first, then Afrikaans, then isiZulu (KZN fee-paying public), then isiXhosa and Sesotho. English isn't enough beyond SA's private schools.

## Risks
- Promise vs outcome: measure something concrete, e.g. the % who understand a topic's 20 key words.
- POPIA: a class code plus word lookups can count as personal information.
- Hidden competition.

## Avoid
Nigeria, Egypt, India, Brazil.

## Final recommendation
South Africa only for the first 6 months, 5–10 schools via ISASA, English + Afrikaans, then UAE or Canada.
