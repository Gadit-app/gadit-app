## Languages
1. **English:** full content and the main UI.
2. **Arabic:** an explanation layer. Arabic UI, explanations and curriculum are separate projects.
3. **Afrikaans:** only after traction in SA.
4. **isiZulu:** a test in SA. Choose by anonymous telemetry of which explanation language pupils pick.
5. **Chinese, Malay, Tamil:** only if Singapore becomes a target.
6. **Dutch:** later.

Build no separate national curriculum before 10 paying schools or a distribution partner.

## First schools
Order: bilingual/international English-medium → independent schools with EAL → EAL/newcomer classes → Jewish diaspora schools (a pilot network) → public schools.

Message: "the teacher projects the lesson's key word; the whole class understands it; the principal sees which concepts still block learning."

## South Africa
- **Content model:** grade/subject → term/topic → keyword with definition, visual, example, misconception and quiz → language explanation. Tag content CAPS-aligned or IEB-aligned; IGCSE/IB schools need other packs.
- **Target list:** 30 schools (Gauteng 15, Western Cape 10, KZN 5), English-medium, CAPS or CAPS/IEB, with learning support, projectors and a deputy head for teaching and learning.
- **Channels:** ISASA, SAHISA (heads), SchoolNet South Africa (non-profit; pilot partnership), InnovateEDU and PD events, then networks via the head of academics.
- **Demo:** the same word across Natural Sciences, Social Sciences and English HL.
- **Pilot:** one month, no student accounts. Collect testimonials and ask each school for 2 intros. Webinar: "how to find vocabulary gaps across subjects without testing every pupil".
- **Pricing:** consider annual billing in rand, tiered, with multi-school discounts.

## UAE plan
- **Prospects:** 40 schools from the KHDA directory.
- **Pitch:** a 20-minute demo, then a 6-week pilot in one class with three deliverables: words mastered, class dictionary growth, and a misconception report.
- **Closing:** close 3 at a founder price in return for a case study and 2 intros. Approach networks (GEMS) only after that.

## Australia plan
- One state (VIC or NSW), 30 independent schools.
- An ST4S-style privacy pack: data inventory, retention, deletion, subprocessors, DPA.
- Run workshops on subject vocabulary, not on "AI".

## Risks
- Building content before sales.
- Privacy: class codes and lookups may still count as student-related data, so the design needs pseudonymous use, auto-deletion and a DPA.
- Selling to the teacher while the principal signs.
- A crowded quiz/flashcard field: the edge is curriculum + projector + class dictionary + principal dashboard.
- Curriculum drift: version the content.
- Teachers may expect translation: promise "multilingual explanation support".

## Before a big investment
Do 15–20 discovery interviews in each of the top three markets.
