Market facts:
- **England:** EAL is 21.4% of state school pupils in 2024/25 (23.4% in primary), 1.75M pupils in 2023.
- **Australia:** ~31% come from a language background other than English; EAL/D reaches up to 90% in some schools.
- **Germany:** DigitalPakt 2.0 is €5bn to 2030, but there are 16 Länder.

## Languages
- Invest in English deep, once.
- Afrikaans only on demand: 3–5 schools or an anchor client.
- Arabic only if the UAE is proven.
- isiZulu and isiXhosa as student explanation languages now, but no full UI or content in them. There are 11,948 MTbBE schools (2025).

Build an "English academic vocabulary spine":
- Tier 1: common words.
- Tier 2: cross-subject academic words (compare, justify, evidence...).
- Tier 3: subject words.
- On top: curriculum-mapping layers (CAPS Grade 4 NS, KS2 Science, AC Year 5).

## First customer (ICP)
An English-medium independent primary or middle school with 300–1,200 pupils, a noticeable multilingual/EAL share, projectors, and a principal or literacy lead who can approve a small pilot.

Customer order: independent → international → bilingual/EAL-heavy → small trusts → Jewish diaspora (a trust channel, but too narrow) → fee-paying public → public. Before going public, have 10–20 success stories, a usage metric, a privacy pack and an impact report.

## South Africa
- **Grades:** start with Grades 4–7, where the shift to academic English bites.
- **Content MVP:** NS&Tech 4–6, Social Sciences 4–6, Maths academic vocabulary 4–7, English FAL 4–7. That's 150–250 words per subject per grade, and at most 1,500–2,000 words at launch.
- **IEB:** works within CAPS. Add an "extension" layer.
- **Target schools:** ISASA (900+) first. Fee-paying public (quintiles 4–5, SGB approval) second. No-fee public only through an NGO, CSI or a funder.

### Offer
- An 8-week pilot for 3–5 classes in Grades 4–7 in one subject.
- Free for the first 10 only, in return for data and a testimonial; otherwise $250–500 ($300, credited against the annual plan).
- Then $1,200 a year for a small school, $1,800 for a large one, billed annually.

### Messages
- "A CAPS vocabulary layer for every classroom."
- "Teach CAPS vocabulary in English; let every learner understand the meaning in the language they know best."

### POPIA package
- No names or emails.
- Pseudonymous session ids.
- A class-level dashboard.
- A DPA/operator agreement.
- A privacy notice.
- Principal can delete and export.
- Raw search events kept 90 days.

### Channels
- Founder-led outreach to 100 ISASA schools in Gauteng, Cape Town and Durban, picked by keywords on their sites (multilingual, EAL, learning support, CAPS, IEB).
- A 60–90 second personal video with one CAPS word and a home-language explanation.
- SAHISA conference (2026: 13–16 Sept, Durban), researchED SA, and a workshop "Teaching the language of CAPS content in multilingual classrooms".
- Distributors (AOLC, Thutho Learning) only after 5–10 paying schools.

### 90-day plan
- 80 schools in 3 lists.
- One landing page: "CAPS Vocabulary for Multilingual Classrooms".
- KPIs: projected lessons per week, word searches per class, and 10–15 hard words before and after.
- Turn 3 pilots into case studies.

## England and Australia
- **England:** London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leicester, Luton, Slough; schools with 25%+ EAL. Packs: KS2 Science, KS2 Geography/History, KS3 Science. Pitch: "Academic language for every subject, without creating student accounts."
- **Australia:** Melbourne and Sydney, AC v9, Year 4–6 Science and Humanities, Year 7 Science.

## Risks
- Overbuilding content: build 3–5 commercial modules.
- A message that's too wide. Use: "Gadit helps every learner understand the academic language of today's lesson."
- Teacher adoption: a 60-second workflow. Key metric: weekly lessons projected.
- Privacy as a trust pack.
- Indirect competition: teacher PDFs, Slides, word walls, "no time for another tool".

## Resource split
- 70% South Africa, 20% England, 10% Australia, all in English.
- No new language before 5 paying schools.
- First content asset: CAPS Grades 4–6. Next: England KS2/KS3 Science.
